Fan-made terminal recreation of the computer from the film WarGames
wopr brings the WOPR (War Operation Plan Response) computer from the 1983
film WarGames to the terminal. Log on at the LOGON: prompt, talk to WOPR,
and play the games on its list: Falken's Maze, Black Jack, Gin Rummy,
Hearts, Bridge, checkers, chess, poker, six war games, tic-tac-toe and
Global Thermonuclear War. Nobody can win Global Thermonuclear War. Movie
mode replays the film's terminal scenes.

It is a single program. It needs no network connection, and it collects no
data. It is not affiliated with the makers of the film.
