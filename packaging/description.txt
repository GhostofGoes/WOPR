Fan-made terminal recreation of the computer from the film WarGames
wopr brings the WOPR (War Operation Plan Response) computer from the 1983
film WarGames to the terminal. Find your way past the LOGON: prompt, talk to
WOPR, and play the games on its list: Falken's Maze, Black Jack, Gin Rummy,
Hearts, Bridge, checkers, chess, poker, six war games, tic-tac-toe and Global
Thermonuclear War, which cannot be won. Movie mode replays the film's
terminal scenes.

It is a single program. It needs no network connection and collects no
data. It is not affiliated with the makers of the film.
